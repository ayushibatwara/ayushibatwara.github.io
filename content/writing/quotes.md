```typst
#quote(block: true, attribution: [Nicholas Carlini \ September 1, 2026 · The Gateway])[
  Researchers are all just gamblers in disguise.
]
```

Carlini argued that there are three possibilities for the future of research with the current world being a combination of all three simultaneously: 

1. Humans as researchers 

2. Models as research assistants

3. Models as researchers

I think the real world today operates at 2.25, where humans are still responsible for steering these "super smart buddies" towards pursuing economically productive ideas.

```typst
#quote(block: true, attribution: [Nicholas Carlini \ September 1, 2026 · The Gateway])[
  Research is dead.
]
```

The bar for what qualifies as a research idea is rising, and Carlini argued that incremental research is no longer worthy of pursuit. This is the same rationale that I operate with – we should be focused on producing ideas that are a step function improvement on the status quo.
