# A Typst math demo

<span class="newthought">This is a sample piece</span> showing Typst math notation. Inline math like $x^2 + y^2 = z^2$ sits right in the text,^[Sidenotes work in pieces too — even with math in them: $e^(i pi) + 1 = 0$.] and display math gets its own line:

$$ integral_0^infinity e^(-x^2) dif x = sqrt(pi) / 2 $$

All of Typst's notation works — sums, fractions, whatever you need:

$$ sum_(k=1)^n k = (n(n+1)) / 2 $$

A literal dollar sign is written `\$`, so prices like \$5 don't turn into math. Delete this piece once you publish your first real one.
